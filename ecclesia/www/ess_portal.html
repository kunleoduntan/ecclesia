🛡️ Information Security Policy v3.0
1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for information security across Ecclesia Group to protect company data, employee information, and client records from unauthorized access or disclosure.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers with access to Ecclesia information systems.

3. Password Management
All system passwords must: be at least 12 characters long; contain uppercase, lowercase, numbers and special characters; be changed every 90 days; never be shared with colleagues.

4. Data Classification
Data is classified as Confidential, Internal, or Public. Confidential data must not be shared outside the organization without written approval from the DPO.

5. Device Security
All company devices must have approved antivirus software installed. Personal devices used for work must be enrolled in the MDM system.
